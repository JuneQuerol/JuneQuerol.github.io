Career Development & Growth Assessment: Strategic Planning for IT Leadership Excellence

Career Development & Growth Assessment: Strategic Planning for IT Leadership Excellence

Abstract

This paper presents a comprehensive career development assessment for a mid-career technology professional who previously served as ICT Head and Part-time Instructor in the Philippines. The evaluation examines demonstrated technical competencies (DevOps, cloud architecture, IT governance), leadership capabilities (infrastructure management, strategic initiatives), and academic contributions (9 scholarly publications spanning governance, cloud migration, and software development). Through systematic analysis of current position requirements, skills inventory, market opportunities, and professional goals, we identify three viable career trajectories: (1) senior IT leadership positions (CIO/CTO), (2) academic research and professorship, and (3) independent consulting with thought leadership. Gap analysis reveals strengths in technical execution and academic writing, with development opportunities in advanced certifications (CISSP, TOGAF), public speaking, and strategic vendor management. The assessment concludes with a 12-month development plan prioritizing Google Scholar profile establishment, conference presentations, and leadership portfolio building, positioning the subject for career advancement into roles commanding 40-60% salary increases while maintaining work-life balance and geographic flexibility.
