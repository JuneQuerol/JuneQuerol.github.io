Publications & Contributions

PACU AI Acceptable Use Policy Development

Philippine Association of Colleges and Universities

March 2024

Role & Contribution

Member of the policy development team for the PACU Handbook on Acceptable Use of AI in Education. Assigned to Group 1: Intentions and Guiding Principles, working alongside national educational leaders to establish the foundational framework for ethical and responsible AI integration in Philippine higher education institutions.

Project Overview

Participated in the landmark PACU writeshop "Harnessing AI Potential: A Writeshop on Acceptable Use in Education" held on March 4, 2024, with over 200 participants including university presidents, academic heads, deans, and industry practitioners from across the Philippines.

Key Contributions

  • Collaborated in developing guiding principles for AI use in educational settings, including enhancement of teaching and learning, ethical compliance, data security, and innovation frameworks
  • Helped establish policy guidelines that balance AI-driven automation with preservation of educator roles and human creativity
  • Contributed to creating a scalable policy template that educational institutions nationwide can customize for their specific contexts

Impact & Reach

The resulting handbook serves as the authoritative reference for PACU member institutions across the Philippines in developing their own AI policies, affecting thousands of educators and students nationwide.
