URL Encoder/Decoder

Encode and decode URLs and URI components

What's the difference?

  • Encode/Decode URL: Encodes the entire URL, preserving URL structure characters like :, /, ?, &
  • Encode/Decode Component: Encodes everything including URL special characters (use for query parameters)

Examples:

Original:
https://example.com/search?q=hello world&lang=en
Encoded URL:
https://example.com/search?q=hello%20world&lang=en
Encoded Component:
https%3A%2F%2Fexample.com%2Fsearch%3Fq%3Dhello%20world%26lang%3Den
